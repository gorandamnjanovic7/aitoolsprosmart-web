💎 1. Extreme 60MP Upscale
------------------------------------------------------------

The engine performs a professional 60 megapixel upscale designed for high-end marketplace delivery, contributor-ready exports, premium advertising assets, and large-format digital production.

Supported final resolutions:

💎 16:9  → 10328 x 5809  = 59.99MP
💎 9:16  → 5809 x 10328  = 59.99MP
💎 1:1   → 7746 x 7746   = 60.00MP
💎 21:9  → 11832 x 5071  = 60.00MP

💎 Technical Insight:
The upscale is powered by premium LANCZOS interpolation, one of the most respected high-quality resampling methods for professional image enlargement. Unlike basic resizing, LANCZOS analyzes surrounding pixel data to rebuild smoother edges, cleaner transitions, stronger structure, and more refined high-resolution detail. This gives the final 60MP export a serious production-grade foundation with reduced softness and fewer jagged artifacts.

💎 Why it matters:
At 60MP, a weak resize method becomes very visible. Edges, gradients, product surfaces, architecture, skin texture, metal, glass, food texture, fabric, and fine detail must remain stable. LANCZOS gives the engine a stronger, more professional enlargement base than simple scaling.

------------------------------------------------------------

The engine uses a mild MedianFilter pass blended back with the original image.

💎 Technical Insight:
Instead of applying a harsh filter across the entire image, the engine creates a cleaned version and blends it carefully with the original. This reduces AI noise and compression artifacts while preserving edge integrity and natural texture.

💎 Benefit:
This helps clean flat surfaces, gradients, and noisy areas without destroying the premium micro-detail needed for contributor approval and commercial trust.

💎 8. Highlight Rolloff
------------------------------------------------------------

The engine uses NumPy matrix processing to soften overly bright areas.

💎 Technical Insight:
Instead of allowing bright pixels to clip harshly into pure white, the engine applies a controlled rolloff to high-value areas. This slows the transition into the brightest zones and helps preserve delicate highlight detail.

💎 Benefit:
This reduces blown-out digital highlights and protects important details in reflections, white products, glass, chrome, jewelry, bright skies, water, food highlights, polished cars, and luxury surfaces.
