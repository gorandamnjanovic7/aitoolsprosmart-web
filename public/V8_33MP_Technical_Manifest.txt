💎 1. Extreme 33.2MP Upscale
------------------------------------------------------------

The engine performs a professional 33.2 megapixel upscale designed for high-end marketplace delivery and contributor-ready exports.

Supported final resolutions:

💎 16:9  → 7680 x 4320  = 33.17MP
💎 9:16  → 4320 x 7680  = 33.17MP
💎 1:1   → 5760 x 5760  = 33.17MP
💎 21:9  → 8808 x 3775  = 33.24MP

💎 Technical Insight:
The upscale is powered by premium LANCZOS interpolation, one of the most respected high-quality resampling methods for professional image enlargement. Unlike basic resizing, LANCZOS analyzes surrounding pixel data to rebuild smoother edges, cleaner transitions, and stronger high-resolution structure. This gives the final 33.2MP export a more refined, marketplace-ready foundation with reduced softness and fewer jagged artifacts.

💎 Why it matters:
For contributor platforms and commercial clients, resolution alone is not enough. The upscale must preserve structure, edges, texture, and visual clarity. LANCZOS provides a stronger professional base than simple resize methods.

💎 8. Highlight Rolloff
------------------------------------------------------------

The engine uses NumPy matrix processing to soften overly bright areas.

💎 Technical Insight:
Instead of allowing bright values to clip harshly into pure white, the engine applies a controlled rolloff to high-value pixels. This slows the transition into the brightest zones and helps preserve highlight texture.

💎 Benefit:
This reduces the blown-out digital look and protects important details in reflections, bright skies, product highlights, glass, chrome, jewelry, and luxury materials.

💎 10. sRGB Marketplace Export
------------------------------------------------------------

The engine performs sRGB marketplace export for safer digital distribution.

💎 Technical Insight:
sRGB is the standard color space for web, stock platforms, contributor websites, mobile displays, browsers, and most client-facing digital environments. The engine attempts to convert embedded ICC profiles into sRGB and falls back safely to RGB conversion when needed.

💎 Trust Signal:
